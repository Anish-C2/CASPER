CASPER Football

Small-sided outdoor football archive. Separate from the futsal pyramid.

Match format

On the pitch

4 versus 4. Each side may name 2 substitutes (6 players in a match-day squad).

Ground

25 m × 15 m. Goals at the 15 m ends.

Scoring

Goals only. Standings: P W D L GF GA GD Pts (3 / 1 / 0).
