Governance

From CASPER, the competitive athletics archive

CASPER is governed globally by a three-member Governance Body. Individual sectors manage their own local activity, within CASPER's laws and competition limits.

Decision making

  • General decisions are taken by the Founder, Neutral and Representative. A majority of the three is required.
  • Major CASPER changes are voted on by every Host together with the three members. A majority is required.
  • No appeals. Disciplinary decisions are final.

Sector governance

Sectors manage their own intra-sector activity. Every sector has a Host. Hosts must operate within CASPER's global laws, and they vote on major structural changes.

Discipline

  • Yellow cards are recorded. Accumulation may trigger a Black Card.
  • Red cards carry an automatic 1-match ban.
  • Black cards. Football: 3-match ban. Futsal: opponent +2 goals; player starts each of the next 3 matches 1 goal down.
