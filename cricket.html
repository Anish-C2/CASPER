CASPER Cricket

Limited-overs cricket archive. Scores are runs and wickets over counted balls, not football goals.

What is stored

Runs

Total runs in the innings, including extras.

Wickets

Batters out. All out is usually 10, or the batting numbers if fewer.

Balls and overs

6 legal balls = 1 over. 18.3 means 18 overs and 3 balls = 111 balls.

NRR

(runs scored ÷ overs faced) − (runs conceded ÷ overs bowled).

How NRR is calculated here

  1. Overs become a real number: balls ÷ 6. So 19.2 = 19 + 2/6 = 19.333…
  2. If a side is all out before the quota, they still face the full quota of overs for NRR.
  3. Table order: points, then NRR, then wickets taken, then runs scored.

CSN match line

home-away:RUNS/WKTS(OVERS.BALLS)-RUNS/WKTS(OVERS.BALLS)#STAGE

Example:
bbu-kko:156/4(20.0)-142/10(18.3)#GS

Set ov=20 in the tournament so all-out NRR uses the correct quota. Points: win 2, tie 1, loss 0.
