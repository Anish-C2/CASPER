CASPER

CASPER is the competition archive, statistics database and historical record system for school sports at one organisation. Results are stored in CSN (Competition Serialization Notation) and rendered in the browser. No live APIs. No duplicated tables.

Sports

Futsal

Indoor archive. The original CASPER competition set lives here: Pioneer Cup, Grande, Premier and the rest of the 2026A pyramid.

Scoring: goals · GD · clean sheets

Football

Small-sided outdoor football. Matches are 4v4 with 2 substitutes on a 25 × 15 m pitch.

Format: 4v4 + 2 subs · Pitch: 25×15 m

Cricket
