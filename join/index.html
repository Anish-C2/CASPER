How to join

From CASPER, the competitive athletics archive

CASPER operates through geographic sectors. Each sector is maintained by a Host who runs competitions and sends results back to the central archive.

What is a sector?

A sector is a geographic operating unit. A street address is not required and must not be published.

Do not publish a street address, house number, venue address, or coordinates.

How to apply

  1. Open a GitHub issue or a discussion.
  2. Identify the Host with a first name or nickname.
  3. Name the sector. Do not include an exact address.
  4. Provide the player roster: nickname, preferred foot, nationality, birth year, and two clubs.
  5. Wait for review. A sector is recognized only after approval.

After approval

  • Host competitions.
  • Keep activity going.
  • Send competition data every week.
