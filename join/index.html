CASPER OPERATES THROUGH SECTORS

CASPER — Competition Archive & Historical Record System — is a distributed, multi-sport competition system. It is operated through geographic Sectors. Each Sector is a remotely operated CASPER location maintained by a Host who runs competitions and submits data back to CASPER HQ.

WHAT IS A SECTOR?

A geographic operating unit

A Sector is a geographic operating unit of CASPER. It is operated remotely from CASPER HQ by a Host who runs competitions and regularly submits competition data to CASPER.

Sectors can operate anywhere. There is no requirement to publish a street address or an exact physical location.

The name is not the address

A Sector’s name is not necessarily its exact geographic location. The Host may combine a location or context with another name or identifier.

Example sector name
Sector 1 — School
Do not publish a street address, house number, venue address, or coordinates.
HOW TO APPLY
1

Contact CASPER

Create a clear Sector application through either GitHub Issues or GitHub Discussions.

Open a GitHub Issue Open a Discussion
2

Identify the Host

Provide a first name or nickname as the Host. That person is the remote operator of the Sector.

Example
Host: Alex
3

Name your Sector

Provide the proposed Sector name. Keep it simple and safe. Identify the Sector without giving an exact address.

Example
Sector 1 — School
Street addresses, house numbers, exact venue addresses, and coordinates are not required and should not be included.
4

Provide the player roster

List every player who will participate in the Sector. For each player include only:

  • Nickname
  • Preferred foot
  • Nationality
  • Birth year (year only, such as 2000 — not a full birthday)
  • Two clubs, including the names of both clubs
Example roster card
Player Preferred foot Nationality Birth year Clubs
Alex Right USA 2000 1. Newland United
2. Park FC
5

CASPER review & approval

CASPER reviews the submitted Sector application. A Sector becomes officially recognized only after approval. Approval is not automatic.

AFTER APPROVAL

Host competitions

The Host must regularly host and run tournaments and competitions in the Sector.

Stay active

Keep competition activity going. A Sector is expected to remain active after it is recognized.

Send data weekly

Submit competition data to CASPER every week so the central archive stays current.

HOST REQUIREMENT

WEEKLY DATA SUBMISSION

Hosts must send their competition data to CASPER every week. That submission keeps the central CASPER archive — statistics, results, records, and historical data — up to date.
