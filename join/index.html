CASPER operates through Sectors

CASPER is a distributed, multi-sport competition system operated through geographic Sectors. Each Sector is maintained by a Host who runs competitions and submits data back to CASPER HQ.

WHAT IS A SECTOR?

A geographic operating unit

A Sector is operated remotely from CASPER HQ by a Host. Sectors can operate anywhere. A street address is not required.

The name is not the address

A Sector name identifies the operating area without publishing an exact location.

Do not publish a street address, house number, venue address, or coordinates.
